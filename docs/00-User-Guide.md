# Users & Self-hosters

Use this guide to get started with Wayfarer, explore your location history and plan
trips. If you run your own server, it also points to installation and normal operation.

## Use the app

- [Getting Started](01-Getting-Started.md): sign in, understand accounts and roles, and find your way around.
- [Features](03-Features.md): explore the capabilities and existing screenshots.
- [Timeline](06-Timeline.md), [Trips](04-Trips.md) and [Groups](05-Groups.md): manage private history, plan travel and share with trusted people.
- [Importing & Exporting](07-Importing-Exporting.md): bring in existing data and export history or trip plans.
- [Mobile App](08-Mobile.md): connect WayfarerMobile for GPS updates and check-ins.

## Install and operate

Start with [Install & Self-Hosting](02-Install-and-Dependencies.md) for supported Linux
platforms, prerequisites and guided Docker/Compose setup. The small `wayfarerctl`
bootstrap acquires and verifies the matching stable deployment for you.

Use [Operate Wayfarer with wayfarerctl](29-Wayfarerctl.md) for everyday status,
diagnosis and logs, plus backup, update and restore procedures.

Review [Security](21-Security.md) for Production password requirements, 2FA,
application protections and trusted proxies. Keep the deployment updated, configure
HTTPS correctly, preserve backups and choose registration and public sharing deliberately.
